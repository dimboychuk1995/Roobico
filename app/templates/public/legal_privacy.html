Roobico

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

Roobico ("Roobico", "we", "us") provides shop-management software for auto repair shops and fleets: work orders, parts inventory, customers, invoicing, payments and a customer portal, available on the web and through our mobile application. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Name, email address, phone number, role and login credentials for users invited to a shop's account.
  • Business records. Data your shop enters while using Roobico: customers and their contact details, vehicles/units, work orders, estimates, invoices, parts and inventory, vendor and payment records.
  • Photos and documents. Images you attach to work orders or units, and photos of paper documents (work orders, vendor invoices) you scan in the app.
  • Billing information. Subscription billing is processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card number on our servers.
  • Technical data. Log data (IP address, browser/device type, timestamps), and — if you enable notifications in the mobile app — a push notification token for your device.

Camera and photo library

The mobile app asks for camera and photo-library access only when you choose to attach a photo or scan a document. Selected images are uploaded to your shop's account and become part of your shop's business records. We never access your camera or photo library in the background.

How we use information

  • To provide and operate the service, including syncing your data across web and mobile.
  • To process subscription payments and send service emails (e.g. invoices, customer authorization requests you initiate).
  • To send push notifications you have enabled (e.g. job assignments, approvals).
  • To secure the service, keep audit records, prevent abuse, and provide support.
  • To improve the product. We do not sell your data and we do not use your business records for advertising.

AI features

Some features use AI to help you work faster — for example, extracting line items from a photographed vendor invoice or a handwritten work order, or polishing text you wrote. The content you submit to these features is processed by our AI service providers solely to return the result to you; it is not used to train their models.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run Roobico — cloud hosting, payment processing (Stripe), email delivery, and AI processing — under agreements that limit their use of your data, and when required by law. Data your shop chooses to send to your customers (estimates, invoices, portal links) is shared at your direction.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your shop's account is active. Individual users can be deactivated by a shop administrator at any time. When a subscription ends, the shop owner may request export or deletion of the shop's data by contacting us; we delete it within a reasonable period, except where we must keep records to meet legal or accounting obligations. Regular encrypted backups are kept on a rolling window and expire automatically.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to production systems is limited to authorized personnel. Each shop's data is stored in a separate database, and access in the application is controlled by per-user roles and permissions.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. If you are an employee or customer of a shop that uses Roobico, that shop controls its business records — please direct requests to the shop first; we will assist them in fulfilling your request. For anything else, contact us directly.

Children

Roobico is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update this page and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: info@roobico.com
