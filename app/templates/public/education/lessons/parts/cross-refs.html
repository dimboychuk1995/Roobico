Where alternates appear

  • The Parts list shows a ⇆ hint next to the part number.
  • Part search on work orders and parts orders lists alternates as extra rows (“⇄ Cross ref for …”) with their own stock — pick the one you have.
  • The mobile app shows them too (⇆ in Parts → Stock).
