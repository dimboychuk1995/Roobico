Transfers between locations move stock without changing the total. One-time parts and “do not track” parts never move stock. Every change is journaled internally.

Negative stock

Work orders deliberately save even without enough stock — you get a warning, the job is not blocked. Fix it by receiving the pending order (the average cost then takes the received price, since stock was zero or negative) or by adjusting the count.

Part History

Parts list → row → {{ ui("History") }}: the orders and work orders that moved this part, with dates and quantities and a date filter. The fastest way to see why a number drifted.
