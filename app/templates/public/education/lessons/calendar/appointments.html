One mechanic, one job at a time

An appointment cannot be saved or moved onto a slot where the same mechanic already has another appointment (cancelled ones do not count) or where the mechanic's timer shows they were actually working on a different work order. The calendar refuses with the conflicting time and work order — pick another time or mechanic. An older appointment that overlaps such actual work gets a red ! badge; fix or delete it.

{% call tip("Turn the plan into a work order") %} Fill customer and unit, then press {{ ui("Create Work Order") }} in the dialog — the work order page opens with everything prefilled and the appointment is linked to it. See {{ lesson_link("calendar/wo-on-calendar") }}. {% endcall %}
