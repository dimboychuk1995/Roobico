Sidebar → {{ ui("Calendar") }} — the shop's appointment board: who is booked for what, and when. Needs {{ perm("calendar.view", "View calendar") }}. It shows appointments — blocks created by hand on the web or in the mobile app. Work orders are not drawn on it by themselves; a work order is on the calendar only through an appointment linked to it ({{ lesson_link("calendar/wo-on-calendar") }}).

{{ shot("calendar-week", "The week view: one column per day, blocks coloured by status, mechanic initials in the corner.") }}

Three views

  • Week — seven columns, Monday to Sunday. Today's number is highlighted; click a day header to open that day in the By mechanic view.
  • By mechanic — one day, a column per mechanic plus Unassigned; each header counts the mechanic's live appointments that day ({{ lesson_link("calendar/by-mechanic") }}).
  • By status — the same one day as a board of three columns: Scheduled, In Progress, Completed. Drag a card into another column to change its status (see below).

In Week and By mechanic the hours run down the left side (hour lines, lighter quarter-hour lines) and a red line marks the current time in today's column. The visible hours come from Working hours in the settings (06:00–20:00 by default) and stretch automatically when an appointment starts earlier or ends later — nothing is ever hidden. A normal day fits the screen; a long one scrolls inside the grid.

What a block shows

  • Colour = appointment status; the legend under the toolbar lists them.
  • First line: the time (07:30–09:00). Then the title in bold — the free-text field for the phone number and the job list; when it is empty the customer's name is shown instead. Then the customer (when it differs from the title) and the unit.
  • In Week view each mechanic of the appointment has a small chip with their initials in that mechanic's colour (top-right corner); the same colour marks their column in By mechanic.
  • A linked appointment carries a WO #123 badge; a Cancelled one stays on the grid, faded and struck through.
  • Two appointments at the same time in one column are drawn side by side — overlaps are allowed, the calendar never refuses them.

Hover a block for the full text.

Toolbar

  • {{ ui("‹") }} {{ ui("Today") }} {{ ui("›") }} — move by week (Week) or by day (By mechanic, By status); the date box jumps straight to a date.
  • {{ ui("Week") }} / {{ ui("By mechanic") }} / {{ ui("By status") }} — switch the view.
  • All mechanics dropdown — show one mechanic, or only unassigned appointments, in any view. The view and the filter are remembered in this browser.
  • ⚙ — calendar settings: statuses, colours and working hours (needs {{ perm("calendar.manage_settings", "Manage calendar settings") }}).

By status board

{{ ui("By status") }} shows the selected day as three columns, each with a counter: Scheduled (everything not started yet — also Confirmed and your own statuses, marked with a small label), In Progress and Completed. The column titles are the status names from the calendar settings. Cancelled appointments are not on the board — a line under the columns says how many; see them in By mechanic or Week.

  • A card shows the time, the title, the customer, the unit, the mechanics and the WO # badge of a linked appointment.
  • Drag a card into another column to change its status (needs {{ perm("calendar.edit", "Edit appointments") }}): into In Progress when the job starts, into Completed when it is done. Esc, or releasing outside the columns, cancels.
  • Only the status changes — never the time, the mechanics or the linked work order. Linked appointments also move by themselves when the mechanic starts or finishes the work order.
  • Click a card to open the dialog; {{ ui("+") }} in a column header adds an appointment straight into that status (needs {{ perm("calendar.create", "Create appointments") }}).
{% call note("The calendar is live") %} Nobody has to reload the page: the calendar checks for changes every 3 seconds, so an appointment created, moved, re-statused or deleted by a colleague, on a phone or by a work order appears on every open calendar within a few seconds. The green Live dot on the right of the legend row shows the page is connected (orange Reconnecting… — the server is unreachable; it recovers by itself). It pauses only while you drag and while the tab is in the background. The address bar follows the date and view, so a bookmark or a link sent to a colleague opens the same day. {% endcall %}
