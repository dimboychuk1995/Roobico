Sidebar → {{ ui("Calendar") }} — the shop's appointment board: who is booked for what, and when. Needs {{ perm("calendar.view", "View calendar") }}. It shows appointments — blocks created by hand on the web or in the mobile app. Work orders are not drawn on it by themselves; a work order is on the calendar only through an appointment linked to it ({{ lesson_link("calendar/wo-on-calendar") }}).

{{ shot("calendar-week", "The week view: one column per day, blocks coloured by status, mechanic initials in the corner.") }}

Two views

  • Week — seven columns, Monday to Sunday. Today's number is highlighted; click a day header to open that day in the By mechanic view.
  • By mechanic — one day, a column per mechanic plus Unassigned; each header counts the mechanic's live appointments that day ({{ lesson_link("calendar/by-mechanic") }}).

In both views the hours run down the left side (hour lines, lighter quarter-hour lines) and a red line marks the current time in today's column. The visible hours come from Working hours in the settings (06:00–20:00 by default) and stretch automatically when an appointment starts earlier or ends later — nothing is ever hidden. A normal day fits the screen; a long one scrolls inside the grid.

What a block shows

  • Colour = appointment status; the legend under the toolbar lists them.
  • First line: the time (07:30–09:00). Then the title in bold — the free-text field for the phone number and the job list; when it is empty the customer's name is shown instead. Then the customer (when it differs from the title) and the unit.
  • In Week view each mechanic of the appointment has a small chip with their initials in that mechanic's colour (top-right corner); the same colour marks their column in By mechanic.
  • A linked appointment carries a WO #123 badge; a Cancelled one stays on the grid, faded and struck through.
  • Two appointments at the same time in one column are drawn side by side — overlaps are allowed, the calendar never refuses them.

Hover a block for the full text.

Toolbar

  • {{ ui("‹") }} {{ ui("Today") }} {{ ui("›") }} — move by week (Week) or by day (By mechanic); the date box jumps straight to a date.
  • {{ ui("Week") }} / {{ ui("By mechanic") }} — switch the view.
  • All mechanics dropdown — show one mechanic, or only unassigned appointments, in either view. The view and the filter are remembered in this browser.
  • ⚙ — calendar settings: statuses, colours and working hours (needs {{ perm("calendar.manage_settings", "Manage calendar settings") }}).
