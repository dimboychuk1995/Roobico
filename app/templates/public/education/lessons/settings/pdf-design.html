Settings → {{ ui("PDF Design") }} (needs {{ perm("settings.manage_pdf_design", "Manage PDF design") }}). Per shop, with a live preview on the right.

{{ shot("settings-pdf-design", "PDF Design with the live preview.") }}
  • Colors & Branding — header and accent colors; Show logo (the logo itself is uploaded on Settings → Shops).
  • Customer & Unit Info — toggles for customer email / phone, unit number, VIN, mileage.
  • Services & Parts — labor hours, rate code, parts detail, core and misc charges, shop supply, paid & balance due.
  • Footer — a thank-you message and terms / notes printed at the bottom.

Click {{ ui("Save PDF Design") }}. Download any work order's PDF to check the result; estimates use the same design with the title Estimate.

{% call tip("Hide what the customer does not need") %} Fleet customers often prefer invoices without hours and rate codes; private customers like to see the parts detail. Toggle accordingly — each shop has its own design. {% endcall %}
