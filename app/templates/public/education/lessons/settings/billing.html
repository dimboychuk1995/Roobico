Pricing

  • Free tier: the first active location and the first active full user cost nothing — forever. A solo shop pays $0 and is never blocked.
  • Beyond that: $100/mo per extra active location, $50/mo per extra active full user, $25/mo per active mechanic (Mechanic and Senior mechanic roles).
  • Only active locations and users count — deactivated staff is free.
  • The 30-day trial has no limits at all.

Example: 1 location, an owner and 2 mechanics = 2 × $25 = $50/mo. 2 locations, 3 office users, 4 mechanics = $100 + 2 × $50 + 4 × $25 = $300/mo.

Annual billing — save 20%

{{ ui("Switch to annual") }} in the Monthly price card: billed once a year at 12 × the monthly price minus 20% (e.g. $50/mo → $480/yr), each payment extends the subscription by a full year, and the price is recalculated from the active locations / users at each renewal. Switch back to monthly any time — the change applies to the next invoice.

Paying

The page shows the subscription status (Active / Renews soon / Payment failed / Expired), the “Paid until” date, the saved card, the invoice history and {{ ui("Pay now") }} (Stripe). Renewal is charged automatically about 3 days before the period ends; a failed charge gets a 7-day grace.

{% call warn("When a paid subscription expires") %} Only the owner can log in — straight to this page — until it is renewed; other users are blocked.
