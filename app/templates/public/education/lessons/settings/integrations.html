In Employees in uAttend tick the people whose time counts and set their $/hr rate. Employees are AI-matched to Roobico users so nobody is counted twice — same name or email in both systems helps.

{% endcall %} {% call step(3, "Where the hours go") %}
  • Timecard / Salary report — hourly pay = punches × rate.
  • General Revenue — payroll in the cash view.
  • Dashboard — the uAttend line in the hours chart.
  • Mechanic Hours report — the uAttend Hours column.

Parts orders from email

One switch, Create parts orders from vendor emails. Turning it on shows the location's private address (orders-<code>@roobico.com) with forwarding instructions for every mail provider, the Suggest only option and the Recent emails history. Issue a new address if the old one leaked to spammers. The full flow is in {{ lesson_link("purchasing/email-orders") }}.
