Live vs pinned prices

By default prices are live: each time a template is shown or applied, each part's price recomputes from its current selling price or the pricing scale. If you edit a price by hand, it becomes a pinned price — highlighted in the editor, saved with the template and used on work orders exactly as typed, beating the part's selling price and the scale. Two things still beat it: a customer with “Override part selling price”, and editing the price on the work order line. To un-pin, clear the price field and click away. Costs always stay live.
