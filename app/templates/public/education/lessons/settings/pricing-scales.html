Settings → Parts & Pricing → card Margin / Markup Rules. Parts without a fixed selling price are priced from average cost through the scale.

{{ shot("settings-pricing-rules", "The Margin / Markup Rules card.") }}

Mode

  • Margin — price = cost ÷ (1 − %); the percentage must be below 100.
  • Markup — price = cost × (1 + %).

Rules by cost range

From / To / %. Leave To empty for ∞. Example one-rule scale: From 0, To empty, 20 → 20% on everything. The seeded “Default” scale is markup-mode with tiers from 500% (parts under $1) down to 15% (over $5000). {{ ui("Add range") }} adds a row, {{ ui("Auto-fill next From") }} closes gaps, {{ ui("Save") }} stores the scale.

Sales Tax Rate

On the same page: automatic by the shop's ZIP code, or a Custom Rate (Override) with {{ ui("Reset to API") }}. The rate is locked into each work order at save time; labor is never taxed.
