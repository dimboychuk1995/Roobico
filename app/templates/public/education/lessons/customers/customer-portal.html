  • Sending a portal link from the customer page.
  • What the customer sees — and cannot do — in the portal.
  • Link validity and renewal.
{% endcall %}

The customer portal is a read-only cabinet where your customer sees their work orders, invoices, payments, vehicles, recall checks and quarterly maintenance files. Approving or declining work does not happen in the portal — that goes through the separate authorization email ({{ lesson_link("work-orders/authorizations") }}); the portal only shows the history.

{% call step(1, "Send the link") %}

Customer page → {{ ui("Portal Link") }} → enter one or more emails → send. Needs {{ perm("customers.manage_portal", "Manage customer portal links") }}. The customer receives a “Your Customer Portal” email.

{{ shot("customer-portal-link-prompt", "Portal Link: enter the recipient emails.", "sm") }} {% endcall %} {% call step(2, "What the customer sees") %}
  • Work Orders — with totals, paid and balance; each opens a detailed view with Download PDF.
  • Vehicles — clicking one filters the other tabs; a per-vehicle Recalls check.
  • Payments — their payment history.
  • Authorizations — approvals and declines they gave by email.
  • Maintenance Files — a quarterly maintenance PDF per vehicle: pick Quarter + Year → Download PDF.

The portal states “This is a read-only customer portal. To make changes, please contact the shop.”

{% endcall %} {% call step(3, "Validity") %}

The link is valid 30 days and renews itself while it is in use. Re-sending usually keeps the same link, so you can simply send it again if the customer lost the email.

{% endcall %} {% call note("Every invoice email carries the link too") %} When you email a work order PDF, the message includes the customer's portal link — so most customers find the portal without a separate invitation. {% endcall %}
