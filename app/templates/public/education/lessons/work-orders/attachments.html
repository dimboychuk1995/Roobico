Three levels

  • Work order — {{ ui("Work Order Attachments") }} at the bottom of the page: inspection photos, the signed paper copy, the customer's purchase order.
  • Labor block — {{ ui("Labor Attachments") }} under each job: before / after photos of that specific repair. Mechanics upload here from the phone.
  • Payment — inside the Record Payment dialog: a check photo or card slip tied to that payment.
{{ shot("wo-attachments", "Work Order Attachments expanded.") }} {% call step(1, "Upload") %}

Expand the attachments block and click the upload button; choose photos, PDFs or other files (up to 16 MB each). Needs {{ perm("attachments.upload", "Upload attachments") }}.

{% endcall %} {% call step(2, "View") %}

Thumbnails open the file in a new tab. Viewing needs {{ perm("attachments.view", "View attachments") }}. Customers see work order attachments in their portal.

{% endcall %} {% call step(3, "Delete") %}

The × on a thumbnail removes the file; it appears only with {{ perm("attachments.delete", "Delete attachments") }}. All attachments are deleted together with the work order.

{% endcall %} {% call note("Other records have attachments too") %} Customers (Details tab), units (Files button), parts (edit form), vendors, parts orders, payments on parts orders and vendor returns each have their own attachments block. {% endcall %} {% call tip("Photos from the phone") %} In the mobile app every job card has a camera / gallery button; the files land in that job's Labor Attachments and are visible on the web immediately. {% endcall %}
