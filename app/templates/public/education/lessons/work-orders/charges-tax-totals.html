Taxable

The Taxable toggle on the work order defaults from the customer's Taxable flag. Tax applies to parts and taxable misc charges only — labor is never taxed. The rate comes from the shop ZIP code (or the custom rate in Settings → Parts & Pricing → Sales Tax Rate) and is locked into the work order at save time, so a later rate change never alters an existing invoice.

{{ shot("settings-sales-tax", "Where the tax rate is configured: Settings → Parts & Pricing.", "md") }}

The formula

LineMade of
Labor totalΣ labor lines (hours × rate, or the manual Labor Total) + shop supply — on the work-order page. On the invoice/PDF, in emails, the customer portal and authorizations the Labor line is labor only; shop supply is its own line (see below).
Parts totalΣ parts (price + charged cores) × qty + misc charges — on the work-order page. Customer documents list Parts (parts only), Core Charges and Misc as separate lines.
Sales tax(parts + taxable misc) × the locked rate, when Taxable is on.
Grand totalLabor total + Parts total + Sales tax.
BalanceGrand total − payments recorded.

Shop supply

A percentage of labor (default 5%, configurable in Settings → Work Order Settings). It shows as an editable Shop supply: $ field with a ↺ reset, and on the work-order page it is part of the Labor total. Set it to 0 for a specific job if needed.

What the customer sees. On the invoice/PDF, in the work-order email, in the customer portal and on authorization pages every amount is listed exactly once: the Labor line is labor only, the Parts line is parts only, and Core Charges, Shop Supplies, Misc and Sales Tax are each their own line before the Total — so Labor + Parts + Core Charges + Shop Supplies + Misc + Sales Tax = Total. Per-job subtotals on those documents are labor + parts (with that job's cores and misc) without shop supply, so job subtotals + Shop Supplies + Sales Tax on taxable work orders add up to the Total. If Settings → PDF Design hides the Shop Supplies line, the amount is folded back into Labor (including the Labor item rows) and the job subtotals on the PDF; hidden Core Charges / Misc lines fold into Parts the same way — so the PDF still adds up to the same Total.

Misc charges

Added with {{ ui("+ Add Misc Charge") }} or carried automatically by catalog parts. Each has its own Taxable checkbox, so a disposal fee can be taxed while a mileage fee is not.
