Locks

  • Paid work orders cannot be edited until made unpaid.
  • Confirmed work orders (a manager confirmed the mechanic's work from the mobile app) are locked for mechanics only — the office can still edit.
  • Mechanics can never set a status themselves: every mechanic save goes to In Progress.
{% call warn("Reverting a paid work order deletes its payments") %} Switching a Paid work order back to Unpaid or In Progress removes all its payment records. The confirmation warns about this, and the action always needs {{ perm("work_orders.change_status", "Change work order status") }} — even for users who may otherwise save work orders. Re-record the payment after editing. {% endcall %}

Deleting

{{ ui("Delete") }} (needs {{ perm("work_orders.delete", "Delete work orders") }}) returns the work order's parts to inventory and removes its payments. The Activity Journal keeps the record of who deleted it.

Mechanic done and Confirmed

These are badges, not statuses. Mechanic done appears when every mechanic who worked on the job pressed Done; the status stays In Progress until you complete it. Confirmed appears after a manager confirms the work from the mobile app. Details: {{ lesson_link("mechanic/done-confirm") }}.
