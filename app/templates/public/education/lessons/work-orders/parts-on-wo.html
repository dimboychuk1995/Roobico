Type three or more characters in the Part Number cell. The dropdown shows catalog results with stock, average cost and cross-reference alternates (“⇄ Cross ref for …”) so you can pick an interchangeable part when the exact one is out.

{{ shot("wo-parts-search-dropdown", "Part search dropdown with stock, cost and the One-time part option.") }} {% endcall %} {% call step(2, "Or add a one-time part") %}

The dropdown always offers {{ ui("+ One-time part") }} — a manual line with no inventory link: editable cost, never deducted from stock. Use it for parts bought for this job only.

{% endcall %} {% call step(3, "Quantity, price, core") %}

Set the quantity; the price fills in automatically (see below). On parts with a core charge a Core toggle appears: line total = (price + core) × qty. If you do not charge the core, the quantity is tracked in the shop's Cores list to send back to the vendor ({{ lesson_link("parts/cores") }}).

{% endcall %}

Where the price comes from

  1. Customer has Override part selling price → always computed from cost via the customer's pricing scale.
  2. Else the part's fixed Selling price, if set.
  3. Else average cost through the pricing scale (margin or markup).

A price you edit by hand is never overwritten. Details: {{ lesson_link("customers/customer-pricing") }}.

Inventory

Stock is deducted when the work order is saved (one-time and “do not track” parts excluded). Stock may go negative — the work order always saves and shortages appear as warnings; fix them by receiving the pending order or adjusting the count. Deleting a work order, or turning it into an estimate, returns its parts to inventory.

Misc charges

{{ ui("+ Add Misc Charge") }}: description, quantity, price and a Taxable checkbox — for shop fees, disposal, freight. Parts can also carry their own automatic misc charges from the catalog (e.g. an environmental fee on every oil filter).
