A service template (preset) is a reusable job: name, labor hours and rate, a parts list and an “Allow customer discount on parts” flag. Templates are built in Settings → Service Templates ({{ lesson_link("settings/service-templates") }}).

{% call step(1, "Open the labor block menu") %}

In edit mode, click ⋮ on the labor block that should receive the job and choose {{ ui("Add Preset") }}.

{{ shot("wo-labor-menu", "Add Preset in the ⋮ menu.") }} {% endcall %} {% call step(2, "Pick the template") %}

Choose a template in the dialog. Its description, hours and rate go into the block and its parts are added to the parts table with live catalog prices.

{{ shot("wo-add-preset-modal", "Add Preset dialog.", "md") }} {% endcall %} {% call step(3, "Adjust and save") %}

Change quantities or hours as needed. Jobs applied from a preset keep the preset's hours instead of auto-filling from mechanic timers.

{% endcall %}

Prices self-heal

Template prices are live: each time a template is applied, each part's price is recomputed from its current selling price or the pricing scale, so templates follow the catalog automatically. If a price was edited by hand in the template editor, it is pinned and used exactly as typed — it beats the part's selling price and the pricing scale. Two things still beat a pinned price: a customer with “Override part selling price”, and editing the price directly on the work order line.

{% call tip("Mechanics use templates too") %} In mechanic mode and the mobile app, “From preset” adds the same jobs and parts — without showing prices. {% endcall %}
