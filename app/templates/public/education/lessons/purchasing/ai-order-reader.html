Price = unit price, never the line amount

The reader extracts two numbers per line — the net unit price and the printed line amount — and checks that price × qty equals the amount. If AI put the line amount into the price (qty 3, amount $150 → “price” $150), it is corrected to amount ÷ qty ($50) before you see it; such a price shows a tooltip on hover. It uses the net (discounted) unit price and the Shipped quantity, and ignores tax, freight and signature lines.
