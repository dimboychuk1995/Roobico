Reports → Mechanic Hours — the one report every role with {{ perm("reports.view", "View reports") }} can open; it contains no money.

{{ shot("report-mechanic-hours", "Mechanic Hours.") }}
ColumnSource
Billed HoursLabor hours from work orders, allocated to mechanics by the assignment percentages set with the Assign button.
Tracked HoursReal job timer time from the mobile app / mechanic page.
uAttend HoursAttendance from the time clock for the same period — shown only when the uAttend integration is connected. Employees are matched to app users automatically; hours of an employee matched to a mechanic land in that mechanic's row, employees matched to non-mechanics are excluded, unmatched employees get their own rows.
WO / labor countsHow many work orders and labor entries the mechanic touched.
{% call tip("Is a mechanic efficient?") %} Compare Billed to Tracked: above 100% you bill more than the time spent. A mechanic who never presses the job timer still shows uAttend hours — you see who was at work even when Tracked is empty. {% endcall %}
