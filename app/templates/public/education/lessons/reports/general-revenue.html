Reports → General Revenue (needs {{ perm("reports.view_financial", "View financial reports") }}). Pick a period and click Generate.

{{ shot("report-general-revenue", "The General Revenue report.") }}

Two headline blocks

  • Revenue − Parts Cost — all income minus the shop's cost of parts used on work orders (payroll not subtracted). The “job view”.
  • Revenue − Parts Orders − Salaries — all income minus real vendor spending minus payroll. The “cash view”.

Under them the same formulas are printed with the actual numbers, then an Activity strip (work orders, parts orders, hours billed, mechanic hours, weeks).

The detail table

Every row has a “What is this” explanation. Groups:

  • Money In — labor, parts, cores, misc, tax → Total Revenue (sales tax collected is included).
  • Money Out — Parts Orders — vendor orders in the period (confirmed ones; returns subtract).
  • Money Out — Payroll — salaries × weeks plus uAttend hourly, per employee.
  • Bottom Line — the Cash view and the Job view.
  • Mechanic Hours — billed vs tracked.

Why is payroll $0?

  • Salary users need Pay type = Salary with a weekly amount (Settings → Users).
  • Hourly users must be ticked in Settings → Integrations → uAttend with an $/hr rate; without the integration there are no hourly hours.
  • A customer filter is applied — payroll and parts orders cannot be split per customer, so they are counted as $0 and a banner explains it.
{% call note("Revenue looks too high?") %} Total Revenue includes collected sales tax, and sales reports date work orders by their Work Order Date. Work orders without a customer are excluded from per-customer reports. {% endcall %}
