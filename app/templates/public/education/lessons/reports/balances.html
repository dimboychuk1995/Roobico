Customer Balances

Per customer: Billed / Paid / Outstanding. Outstanding is floored at 0 per work order — an overpaid work order never offsets another. The same number appears on the Customers list (Current Balance column), on the customer page and as the dashboard's Outstanding Balance widget.

Vendor Balances

Per vendor: Orders, Total, Paid, Outstanding — for confirmed parts orders. Credits from returns never push a balance negative.
