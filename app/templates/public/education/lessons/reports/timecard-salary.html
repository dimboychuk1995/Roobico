How rows are computed

  • Salary employees: weekly salary amount × weeks in the period (days ÷ 7; a shorter range prorates fractionally). Pay type and amount are set in the user form (needs {{ perm("settings.manage_pay", "Edit employees' pay type & salary") }}).
  • Hourly employees: uAttend punches in the period × their hourly rate (set in Settings → Integrations; only employees ticked there appear).
  • uAttend people are auto-matched to Roobico users so nobody is counted twice: an employee matched to a salary user with a weekly amount is folded into that row with a “↔ uAttend” badge; one matched to an hourly user — or to a Salary user with no amount — keeps their own hourly row with the user's role and a “↔ Internal” badge.
  • A note “uAttend rate … is not used — Pay type is Salary” means the person has both a weekly salary and an hourly rate: the salary is paid. If an hourly rate was typed into “Salary amount” by mistake, switch Pay type to Hourly in Settings → Users.

Periods: This week / Last week / This month / Last month / This year / Custom. Banners explain when the uAttend integration is missing, disabled or erroring. General Revenue uses this same calculation, so its payroll equals the totals here for the same From / To dates (its uAttend hours can be up to 15 minutes old).

Hours by day

The table shows one total per employee. A row with uAttend punches in the period has a small {{ ui("›") }} arrow in front of the name — click it to open the days under the row: the weekday and date, every clock-in – clock-out pair (a lunch break gives two pairs) and the hours of that day. {{ ui("Show daily hours") }} above the table opens every employee at once; {{ ui("Hide daily hours") }} folds them back. Nothing is open when the page loads, and the days simply add up to the row's Hours.

  • Hover a pair for its length, the uAttend department and how the punch was made.
  • A pair with a pencil on a yellow background was corrected by hand in uAttend.
  • “08:05 – on the clock” — the person has not clocked out yet; the day counts 0 hours until they do.
{% call tip("Wrong hours? Fix them in uAttend") %} Roobico only reads uAttend — its API has no way to add or change a punch. Correct the punch on the employee's timecard in uAttend and reload this report: it always asks for fresh punches, so the day and the pay are recalculated at once. {% endcall %} {% call note("Nobody can change their own pay") %} Even with the pay permission, a user cannot edit their own pay type or salary — an owner's pay is edited from another owner account, or not at all. {% endcall %}
