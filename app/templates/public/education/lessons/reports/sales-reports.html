All three need {{ perm("reports.view_financial", "View financial reports") }} and use the standard filters (date preset, group by, customer / vendor multi-select).

Sales Summary

Billed totals per customer for the period: Orders, Labor, Parts, Parts Cost, Tax, Hours, Revenue. Work orders are dated by their Work Order Date (falling back to creation date); work orders without a customer and estimates are excluded. The summary adds the average ticket and invoiced hours.

{{ shot("report-sales-summary", "Sales Summary.") }}

Payments Summary

Money received per customer: payment count, amount, average payment — dated by payment date, not work order date.

{{ shot("report-payments-summary", "Payments Summary.") }}

Parts Orders Summary

Vendor spending split by type — Parts, Cores, Shop Supply, Tools, Utilities, Pmt to Svc — with totals, paid and balance per vendor. Orders from email that are not confirmed are excluded; returns subtract.

{{ shot("report-parts-orders-summary", "Parts Orders Summary.") }} {% call tip("Sales vs payments") %} Sales Summary answers “what did we bill this month”; Payments Summary answers “what cash came in this month”. A job billed in March and paid in April appears in March sales and April payments. {% endcall %}
