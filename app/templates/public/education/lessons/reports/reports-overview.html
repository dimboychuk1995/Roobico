The Standard Reports page

The first seven reports open one shared page as tabs. Pick filters and click {{ ui("Generate") }}; {{ ui("Download PDF") }} exports the same report (needs {{ perm("reports.export", "Export reports") }}).

{{ shot("report-filters", "Report tabs and filters: date range, customer filter, Generate, Download PDF.") }}
  • Date Preset: All Time, Today, Yesterday, This Week, Last Week, This Month (default), Last Month, This Quarter, Last Quarter, This Year, Last Year, Custom. Weeks start Monday; dates use the shop timezone.
  • Chart Group By: Month or Week (Sales, Payments, Parts Orders, Mechanic Hours, General Revenue).
  • Customers multi-select (Sales, Payments, Customer Balances, General Revenue) and Vendors multi-select (Parts Orders Summary), with search and Select All / Deselect All.
  • Customer Balances and Vendor Balances are always all-time — no date filter by design.

Cards you are not allowed to open are not shown. Default roles: Owner / General manager / Manager see everything; Parts manager and Viewer see financial reports but not payroll; Senior mechanic and Mechanic see only Mechanic Hours (a mechanic has no Reports section at all).
