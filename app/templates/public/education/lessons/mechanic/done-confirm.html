Done — ready for review

Pressing {{ ui("Done — ready for review") }} does not change the status. It shows managers a green Mechanic done badge (web list, mobile list, work order details) meaning the mechanic finished and the job awaits review. Any new change or a restarted job timer clears the badge.

{{ shot("wo-list", "The Work Orders list — Mechanic done and ✓ Name marks appear under the status badge.") }}

Several mechanics — Done is per mechanic

Everyone who worked on the work order (started a timer on it, or is assigned to one of its jobs) presses Done themselves. Until the last one does, the work order stays in the manager's In Work group, shows no Mechanic done badge and sends no “finished” push; the list shows who already finished as a green ✓ Name. In your own app the work order shows Done as soon as you pressed it — your mark, not the whole job. When a manager saves the work order as In Progress, all marks are cleared.

In the app, {{ ui("Confirm work order") }} after the Mechanic done badge. The work order gets a Confirmed badge and is locked for mechanics — they cannot open, edit or start a timer on it until the manager cancels the confirmation. The status does not change — complete it and collect payment as usual.

Push notifications (office users)

  • “WO #… in work” — a mechanic started their first job timer on it, saved it from mechanic mode, or picked a finished work order back up after Done.
  • “WO #… finished” — the (last) mechanic pressed Done; the work order awaits review.

Tapping the notification opens the work order in the app (switching the active shop first if needed). Notifications are per device: they start after the first login on the phone and stop on logout. Mechanics do not receive them; actions by office users do not trigger them. Requires the installed app (not the Expo Go development client).
