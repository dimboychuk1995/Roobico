The login page

Open roobico.com. The left panel is the login form; the right side describes the product and shows pricing. The same email and password work in the mobile app.

{{ shot("login-page", "The login page: the Log in panel on the left, the product tour and pricing on the right.") }} {% call step(1, "Enter your email and password") %}

Type the email your account was created with and your password. The eye icon shows the password while you type. Click {{ ui("Log in") }}.

{{ shot("login-panel", "The Log in panel with the eye icon, Forgot password and the Register link.", "md") }} {% endcall %} {% call step(2, "Where you land") %}

Owners and managers land on the Dashboard; a user whose role has no dashboard goes to the first section they are allowed to see. Mechanics go straight to the mechanic work order list (see {{ lesson_link("mechanic/mechanic-mode") }}).

{% endcall %} {% call note("Too many attempts") %} After ten failed attempts from one address the login is paused for a few minutes. Wait, then try again, or use Forgot password. {% endcall %}

Forgot password

Click {{ ui("Forgot password?") }} under the login form, enter your email and press the button. If the email is registered, a reset link arrives within a minute; it is valid for one hour. Open it, type the new password twice and log in.

{{ shot("login-forgot-password", "Forgot password: enter your email to receive a reset link.", "md") }} {% call warn("Nobody can set your password for you") %} Passwords in Roobico are strictly personal. Neither the owner nor a manager can see or change another user's password — the only path is the reset link sent to the account email. Invited users set their first password through the invitation email. {% endcall %}

Contact us

The login page has a second tab, {{ ui("Contact us") }}. It shows the support phone number {{ support_phone_display }} (tap to call on a phone) and a short form: your name, email, optional phone and company, a topic (general question, demo, pricing, help with my account, billing) and your message. We reply by email, usually within one business day. The same tab opens from the Contact link in the top navigation and from the footer.

{{ shot("login-contact-tab", "The Contact us tab: phone number card and the message form.", "md") }}

Registering a new shop

{{ ui("Register") }} opens the signup form: company name, phone and address, then the owner's name, email and password. The first location and the first full user are free forever; you get a 30-day trial with no limits on top of that.

{{ shot("login-register-modal", "Create your account: company details and the owner account.", "md") }} {{ shot("login-pricing", "Pricing: free tier, per extra location, per extra user and per mechanic; annual billing saves 20%.", "md") }}

Confirming your email

After signup the owner receives a confirmation email. Until the link is clicked, a yellow bar at the top of the app reminds you, with a {{ ui("Resend email") }} button (the link lives 7 days; resending is limited to a few times per hour). Confirmation does not block any feature — it only verifies we can reach you. Team members confirm automatically when they accept their invitation.

Logging out

The red {{ ui("Logout") }} button sits in the top-right corner of every page. On a shared shop computer, log out when you leave.
