Sidebar

The left sidebar lists every section your role can open: Dashboard, Calendar, Parts, Vendors, Customers, Work Orders, Settings, Reports, Perks, Import / Export and this Education Center. Below the sections sits the {{ ui("Roobico AI Helper") }} button and, at the very bottom, the theme toggle.

{{ shot("sidebar-expanded", "The expanded sidebar with global search, sections, the AI Helper and the theme toggle.", "sm") }}

The hamburger button collapses the sidebar to icons to give tables more room; the choice is remembered in your browser. If a section is missing from your sidebar, your role does not include it — ask the account owner.

{{ shot("sidebar-collapsed", "Collapsed sidebar: icons only.", "sm") }}

Top bar

The top bar shows who is logged in, the organization name, the Active shop dropdown and {{ ui("Logout") }}. Organizations with several locations switch between them here — every page then shows only that shop's customers, parts and work orders. Users only see the shops they were granted access to.

Global search

Themes

The toggle at the bottom of the sidebar switches between the light and dark theme. The choice is saved in the browser, so each computer and each user can have their own.

{{ shot("theme-dark-dashboard", "The dashboard in the dark theme.") }}

Every table is personal

Every table in Roobico can be adjusted per user, and the layout follows you to any device:

  • Hide / show columns — hover the table and click the small ⚙ button in its top-right corner, then tick the columns you need. At least one column always stays visible.
  • Resize — drag the thin divider on the right edge of a column header.
  • Sort — click a header; arrows show the direction. Paginated lists sort on the server across all pages.
  • Reset — ⚙ → {{ ui("Reset table") }} restores the defaults.
{{ shot("table-columns-menu", "The ⚙ table settings menu above the Customers table.") }} {% call tip("Nothing you do here affects colleagues") %} Column choices, widths and sort order are saved to your account only. Other users keep their own layouts. {% endcall %}
